## Review · 2026-03-17 · `3f09b1d`

> **Verdict: 💬 commented (1 major, 1 minor)** · `3f09b1d` → `main` · by @dana · $0.96 · 3m35s · risk: 🟡 medium

Failed webhook deliveries are now retried with exponential backoff instead of being dropped after
the first error.

**Key changes**
- `WebhookDispatcher` schedules retries at 1 m, 5 m, 30 m and 2 h, then marks the delivery failed.
- New `webhook_attempts` table records each try and its HTTP status.

**Findings**
- **major** · `src/webhooks/dispatcher.ts:88` — Retries aren't idempotent: a delivery that timed out
  but actually reached the merchant is sent again without the `Idempotency-Key` header.
- **minor** · `src/webhooks/backoff.ts:12` — No jitter, so a burst of failures retries in lockstep.

**Follow-ups (private)**
- Check that the merchant docs mention the retry schedule.

## Review · 2026-03-18 · `7c41e2a`
