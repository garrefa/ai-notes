# Dishwasher not draining

## Tried so far

- Cleaned the filter under the lower spray arm: lots of gunk, but still not draining.
- Checked the drain hose under the sink: no kinks.

## Next

- Check the air gap / sink connection for a clog.
- If that's clear, it's probably the drain pump. Model: SX-450, bought 2022, so the warranty has
  expired. A replacement pump is about 45 € online vs. about 120 € for a technician visit.
