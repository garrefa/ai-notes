# Plan: balcony herb garden

## Goal

Fresh herbs for cooking and something green to look at while studying.

## Shopping list (bought)

- 3 rail planters (60 cm), potting soil (40 L), drainage pebbles
- Seeds: basil, parsley, chives; seedlings: mint, rosemary

## Layout

- Sunniest rail: basil and rosemary.
- Partial shade: parsley and chives.
- Mint in its own pot (it spreads).

## Outcome

Done over the weekend for 58 €. Water every other morning; basil sprouted after 6 days.
