# Monthly deep-clean routine

What worked this time, so next month takes under 3 hours.

1. **Kitchen (45 min):** oven racks soaking first, then the fridge shelves, then the extractor filter.
2. **Bathroom (40 min):** descale the shower head in vinegar, regrout check, wash the mat.
3. **Bedroom (30 min):** flip the mattress, wash the duvet, vacuum under the bed.
4. **Living room (30 min):** dust shelves top to bottom, wipe switches and door handles.
5. **Finish (15 min):** take the recycling out, replace sponges and the vacuum bag.

Supplies to restock: dishwasher salt, microfiber cloths, descaler.
